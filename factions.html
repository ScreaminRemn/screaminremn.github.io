The Great Seas

The Great Seas appear vast and empty at first, but only a fool would believe such a thing. Below the surface, great fish, whales, krakens, leviathans, and greater leviathans await. In the fogs and clouds, beings of various sorts descend to consume. This is but a small list of these creatures.

Rozjalczy

Rozjalczy are a small kraken-like leviathan. Their forebodies are those of a massive shark, possessing an upper midnight-purple coloration and a lower stark white coloration. Their eyes are an empty, unfathomable orange-yellow. This forebody is a little smaller than a galleon, while their end does not descend into a proper fin. Instead, it descends into a myriad of kraken-like tendrils, used to grab onto prey and ships to subdue them for prompt consumption, using hook-like beaks along either side of these tendrils to dig into prey and ship alike. Due to the coloration of their upper forebodies and tendrils, Rozjalczy are extremely valuable leviathans to hunt. Additionally, much of their sinew can be used for high-quality greases for fire pots used by catapults. Rozjalczy typically swim in colder waters, typically found in the northern portions of Yggrdziel and underneath the Screaming Moon.

Pale Worm

The Pale Worm is one of the smallest leviathan-whale hybrids, though, despite being a leviathan-whale, it is often left alone due to the lack of anything valuable compared to the difficulties provided when catching one. They are only a couple hundred feet long with a mouth large enough to consume just shy of a dozen men. Inside is no tongue, only teeth lining the insides. There are two black voids where eyes should be, with the skin rucked up round about as though the eyes were plucked out long ago. There isn’t much by way of any insides for valuables, but they are surprisingly fast and are extremely aggressive. Rest assured, if you see one, it has already seen you.

Fogworn Glaucus

The Fogworn Glaucus is a drastically larger version of the glaucus atlanticus, nearly the size of two galleons. Instead of the traditional blacks and blues, it is colored with light grays and misty greens, meant to blend into the fog, clouds, and seas that it inhabits. It rests high within the clouds, though none quiet know how, and it primarily glides about. Its face is adorned with two smooth, extremely forwards leaning horns, and in place of a mouth are dozens of mandibles. It feasts primarily on great fish and the carcasses of whales and leviathans that have floated too high to the surface. It’s passive, for the most part, but aggressive when in the fog. The Fogworn Glaucus is worthwhile to hunt due to it’s hide and hollow bones, as the hide is tough yet light, and the bones make for excellent instruments and tools of various kinds. From the horns, it is able to gather moisture and heat in the air and, using an internal organ useful for chemical weapons, it’s able to charge up and let out a pale green bolt of lightning for hunting and territory defense purposes.

False Galleon

The False Galleon is a small mimic-kraken of sorts that calls the seas beneath the Screaming Moon home. It inhabits abandoned or shipwrecked galleons, bringing them to the surface and “sailing” them, though it only uses this to safely get closer to other ships or to lure great fish to it before lunging at and attempting to consume its prey. From afar, it is difficult to tell, but there will be signs that something is amiss.

Qasirpran

The Qasirpran is a great “fish” in the vague shape of a shrimp. It is black with a ghostly teal along the top, a being of the depths, whose innards glow. Upon opening its maw, a baleful light will shine forth, blinding in the darkness of the deep, something that makes the rest of the creature nigh on impossible to see. This light often lures in other great fish and whatever may lurk, though upon getting close enough, it is easy to tell what the source of the light is. However, the Qasirpran possesses extreme burst-swimming speed, unsustainable for long periods of time, and can extend four “inner” jaws, each layered within another, to reach and bite. It calls the depths of the cliffs of southern Fahroway home and the depths three-score thousand leagues north-west of Ekternau home.

Fahroway

As above in Mothua, so below in the Darklands, the creatures here are vast, yet wrong. These are they.

Outer Mimics

Outer Mimics are, most likely, the primary reason that humanity was given the uncanny valley. They are humanoid, but their faces are too stretched and bony, as though the skin is pulled taut over bone. The juveniles are too wrong to be confused for people, but they are flexible, able to hide in the furniture and devices of men in their homes, waiting. They typically target homes on the outer edges of society.

The adults, however, called True Mimics, gain a more human-like appearance, though still visibly wrong. However, they have grown intelligent enough to wear the clothing of the fallen, or, rather, pieces of it, to better aid in their disguise. These ones typically target travellers, Outer Mimics caught in the open, or small outposts.

Ekternau

The Fourthfog Bestiary is a large book, a collection of known creatures residing on the islands of Ekternau. This is just a collection of some creatures that can be found, though not all, as not everything has been documented successfully. It is called the Fourthfog Bestiary for a handful of small reasons, the first being that it is first and foremost a bestiary. The others, fog, for being the bestiary of the foggy islands of Ekternau, and fourth for being the fourth attempt at such a thing. There was once a fifth being produced, though the writer and explorer of it was eaten by a Chrant Mimic before he could finish it.

Ylraz Frog

The Ylraz Frog resides on the island which Jentrybol is located, though they can be found on a few other islands. They look like a strange mix of frog and lilypad, able to blend in as lilypads, which they can take the form of for extended periods of time due to their powerful little lungs. These creatures often will fire their tongues deep into the water, which they focus into a spear-like shape to impale saltfish to bring them up and eat. An Ylraz Frog can cause minor wounds to a person, though it is unwise to sleep near places of their habitats due to the fact that their tongues can pierce the throat. Additionally, it is unwise to eat them, as they are poisonous due to the deadwater being soaked into their skin.

Chrant Mimic

The Chrant Mimic, named after the first person who was eaten by these creatures, Edar Chrant, is a Mimic-type creature. They assume the form of branches of various trees, skin colored to match the tree they attach to. The end of the “branches” split, often, into three or four jaws with a coiled, grabbing tongue, meant to snare any passerby or passing creature to be consumed. However, Chrant Mimics have been seen consuming other Chrant Mimics that have failed to blend in or that are on the move. The Chrant Mimics can be found in the southern islands of Ekternau.

Sedyr

Sedyrs are fanciful, coral-like, serpent-like creatures with small limbs, clawed and webbed little arms in several places round about. They are rather large, the juveniles reaching crossing end-to-end near a score-and-ten feet long. These creatures are proud, territorial creatures, and have often been seen writhing and biting at each other, able to spit out highly pressurized streams of water sharp enough to begin cutting through stone - though it would take a moment to fully cut through. They don’t actually reside on the islands themselves, but rather, in the water pools, rivers, and the coasts. It’s advised to take scorched-metal shields when dealing with them, wait for one to deal with another, or wait for those of the Ekternau Enclave to deal with one and leave part of the carcass behind.

Stiltleg Elk

The Stiltleg Elk are tall, elk-like creatures, though their bodies are a bit smaller. Their legs are tall and oddly thin, having four front legs and two hind legs, allowing them to technically tower over normal elk. Their antlers are not spread out so much to the sides, instead hanging forwards. Their fur is a foggy-green, meant to blend in well to the mist, and their legs and clawed hooves are a bark-black. Stiltleg Elks are fast and quick, surprisingly agile and dextrous, able to skitter over rocks in ponds and lakes, across rivers and streams with ease. They’re more passive, but aggressive if one gets too close. They’re not a good source of meat, due to their thinner, smaller bodies, but they’re better than nothing. Additionally, the clawed hooves aren’t much more dangerous than normal hooves, unable to flex really or grab, only meant to stamp over and hook into various things for better grip.

Stonefisk

Stonefisks are lethal little critters, disguised as small stones, barely the size of twice a fist. Spread apart and dissected, they appear like a mix of crustacean and arachnid, though they have a rocky shell. If their rocky shell is stepped on, they stab a stinger up and into their prey, poisoning them, before the Stonefisk begins to skitter along - at a distance - after the prey. Once said creature falls, the Stonefisk eats its fill and will then hide nearby if the carcass hasn’t been finished off, waiting to eat until later when it hungers again.
