The Anointed Holy Cities

3 completed datasheets in the current document.

Very few among the Anointed Holy Cities are truly powerful, however, what they lack in strength they make up for in sheer numbers. Untold millions of peasants, untold thousands of knights, endless Inquisitors and Holy Sisters, machines of war and great and horrifying siege machines are they. The earth shall be covered in a mass of writhing flesh and metal and misery and the suns shall be blotted out with endless arrow, bolt, and spell.
