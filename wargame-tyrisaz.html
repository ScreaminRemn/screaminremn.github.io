Tyrisaz

5 completed datasheets in the current document.

The Tyrisaz were the first titan-folk to roam the lands, all practically demigods, sons of Ljolnir himself. There are few of them left, but they are among the most mighty and powerful beings that have not ascended to godhood.

This is a High Armor, High Health Army.
