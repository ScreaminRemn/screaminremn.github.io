People of the Far-Dunes

6 completed datasheets in the current document.

There are few among the nomadic tribes of the People of the Far-Dunes, but through rituals and great deeds, they may summon Djinn and Plated Visages. Each Djinn is different, a different being of great power, but any Djinn can be summoned with the right ritual.

Army Rule / Unit Type

Djinn

Djinn cost zero Coins, but they require specific actions to be performed before they can be deployed and used. There are three types of Djinn: Executioner, Undying, and Manipulator. Executioner Djinn focus on offensive power, Undying Djinn focus on defensive power, and Manipulator Djinn are masters of all things strange. However, due to the delicate balance of summoning rituals, only a total of three Djinn can be summoned per match.

When performing an act to summon a Djinn, assuming multiple Djinn require the same act, only one Djinn can be summoned for that one act, though it is whichever the user desires. For example, should one have two Djinn that require the taking of an objective, then upon taking that objective, they can only summon one of the two Djinn, though they can choose which of those two Djinn they want to summon.
