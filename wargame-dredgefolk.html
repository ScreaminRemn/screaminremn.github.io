Dredgefolk

0 completed datasheets in the current document.

Dredgefolk have some piddly guys, some decent guys, some expensive heavy hitters, but their big thing is having big old ships carried along the land by summoned water. These ships are dedicated transports, but don’t let that fool you: they are loaded up with cannons and nets and harpoons and anchor-launchers.
