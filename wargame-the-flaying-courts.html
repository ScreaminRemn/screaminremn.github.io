Army Rule

Court Jesters

Once per Round, one unit can roll 2D6 for one of the following: Upon rolling a 12, this unit gains 1 free Retaliate upon their next Melee Engagement. Upon rolling an 11, this unit gains +2 Actions for the rest of the Round. Upon rolling a 10, this unit gains +1 Holding Power and +1 Might. Upon rolling a 4, this unit loses 1 Holding Power and 1 Might. Upon rolling a 3, this unit loses 1 Action. Upon rolling a 2, all enemy units ignore this unit’s Armor in this unit’s next Melee Engagement. All effects last for 2 rounds unless stated otherwise.
