The World

This world is not Earth. In the far reaches of nothingness, there exists a planet of such abnormal size that it can fit hundreds, perhaps thousands of normal worlds within it with extreme ease, making it one of the largest worlds to exist.

This world has no name, for the untold age of billions and trillions of years and the civilizations that came from it, none of the peoples of this world could agree upon a name. Those of the Anointed Holy Cities refer to it as Grimmvein, those of The Flaying Courts call it the Great Tribute, the Shrikes call it Yggrdziel, and so on. For this retelling, we shall call it Yggrdziel, named so by Those Who Came First, for the sake of ease of communication.

Ruled by several pantheons of the cruel and thirsting laughter of Gods and Goddesses alike, both lesser and greater, no one ruler truly reigns.

This world has dozens of continents, each the size of a world, perhaps multiple, at the very smallest.

None of the rest of the races of the vast emptiness and nothingness choose to come near this world, for it is cursed: this world is frozen in time, stuck in primitive and medieval ages for eons and untold time. Any who come near with more advanced technology always seem to meet an unfortunate end or accident of some kind, without exception.
