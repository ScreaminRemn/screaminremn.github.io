Sädekoti

Jakiisi are a goblin race, once known as Mortal Giants, having devolved over time, residing in dark, snowy mountain villages. They are nearly a man-and-a-half in height, though they often take crouched positions for the purposes of stealth, as they frequently hunt game for food. Their skin is generally a cold, light grey, often bony, with chunky white hair. Their hands, forearms, legs, and feet are rather large, though almost purely muscle, and they are largely clothed in grays and whites to better blend into the dark mist and snow.

They have a more tribalistic society, scattered amongst the mountain ranges of Krashq’kanov, though mostly north of the Obsidian Plains, ruled by chiefs. Each chief becomes one after a trial-by-combat, where the former chief left for the Ættestup to preserve his honor.

Their weaponry is generally sharp and jagged, though mostly using spears, shepherd’s axes, and long bows with stony tips that can be swung to hurt more immediate enemies. They have a scratchy language as well, which appears tied to their magicks, using watered down versions of runes and incantations, incantations being spells that require chanting of sorts, used by Hos-Jakiisi.

They do, however, have a king above all, a Hos-Jakiisi who wields power above even the other Hos-Jakiisi, for he was granted the blessed title of Blade-Saint, a gift from Almictus, for his many prayers to her. The swordspear itself, the weapon gifted to him along with the title, is white, though the blade quickly fades into a fluorescent, glowing yellow. The blade is long and thin with various symbols from the Glos-Lehxikus engraved upon it, each a dark turquoise, a script of Almictus’ own making. As with all Blade-Saints before him, he has been made immortal, living and ageless until he is slain.

This Hos-Jakiisi once had a traditional Jakiisi name, though, in honor of his chosen Goddess, he has renamed himself Hos-Jasander. He wears the traditional white robes and feathers of Jakiisi and Hos-Jakiisi, though now he wears the vibrant, bright red laurel wreath crown, a common symbol of Almictus. Never shall he fall, however, for he resides within the deepest of Jakiisi territory and is a foe more formidable than most can imagine. So elevated is he, of a people already beyond human, of magicks beyond Jakiisi and making him a powerful Hos-Jakiisi, that even he could contend with the likes of Iystrobahths in direct combat.

Sädekoti

The heart of the Jakiisi territory contains a place known to the outside as a crystal keep, though the Jakiisi themselves call it Sädekoti. It is made of towering crystal spyres, awash with softly glowing light, of blues and greens and pinks, while the ice of the mountains grows thick around it, protecting it, shielding it. The path to this keep is treacherous, but the reward is great, for it is a keep, a castle, a fortress, a strange paradise of untold beauty.

Settled near it are various aviary-like stables, each containing specially bred giant vanhr shrikes, great shrikes of white and grey, each large enough for a Jakiisi or Hos-Jakiisi to ride and fly atop the backs of. With these mounts, the Jakiisi and Hos-Jakiisi fly round about their territories, guarding it, and occasionally heading south to scout the territories of the Shrikes to attempt to expand their territory.

Within the central-most spyre of Sädekoti, the greatest and most grand spyre sits, one that pierces the heavens. This central spyre, known as Kiteenpistos, is the home of Hos-Jasander, his throne room, and the Upper Heavens, the gateway to Almictus’ Plane of Worship, where one can commune with Almictus through prayer and devotion. Call, and do so properly, and she shall answer.
