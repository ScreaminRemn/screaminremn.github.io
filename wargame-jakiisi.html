Jakiisi

5 completed datasheets in the current document.

The Jakiisi are great hunters, warriors, and rune seers, as they are a devolved race of giants and have become goblin-folk. They soar on the backs of great Giant Vanhr Shrikes, hailing down arrow and spell, swooping in to lunge forth with a great impaling stab with their spears. Even without their mounts, they are fearsome, being greater than the frail humans, but they aren’t quite elite.

This is a High Action, High Holding Power, High Might Army, and Above Average Health Army..
